players and collectors aren't just competing with scalpers for One Piece product anymore

live-rip sellers (or joki as we call them locally) are becoming another major buyer of sealed product

in an ideal world, products would flow from: 
distro > shops > players & collectors

now it's a larger group of ppl fighting over the same allocation

scalpers remove product from retail and resell it higher

live-rip sellers consume sealed products, break them down into packs then monetise the experience of ripping packs

that changes the market in a few ways:

• less sealed product is available at msrp
• product gets bought up much faster after release
• live-rip sellers can sometimes absorb higher costs because they're not just selling the sealed product

and this isn't just happening locally here. there are different versions of what i see locally across different regions

but the problem is we don't have enough data to know how much each group is affecting supply

1⃣ how much allocation ends up with scalpers?

2⃣ how much product gets bought up for live-rip?

3⃣ how much is left for players & collectors to buy at msrp?

until we have a clear view of these numbers, it's hard to say what's really driving shortages and pushing prices higher especially during pre-release

but one thing is clear

the One Piece TCG market isn't just players and collectors anymore

it's now players, collectors, scalpers and live-rip sellers all fighting over the same supply
